Recognize a stroke FAST

Call emergency services immediately if you notice ANY of these symptoms. Treatment works best when started quickly, so every minute counts.

Guided FAST check

If symptoms started suddenly, call 911 now—do not wait to finish this guide. Check for face drooping, arm weakness, or unusual speech. If you see a change or are unsure, use the emergency button below.

Optional camera mirror—not a stroke testUse the live mirror only to view your face while smiling. This app does not analyze facial movement, expressions, or skin color, and a normal-looking image cannot rule out a stroke. Do not delay emergency care to use the camera.

FaceAsk them to smile. Does one side droop or feel numb?
ArmsAsk them to raise both arms. Does one drift down or feel weak?

Do not wait for an app analysis. A possible or uncertain sudden change needs emergency assessment. Call 911 now

The camera is an optional live mirror only; it does not record or analyze your face. No camera result can rule out a stroke. Sudden symptoms or uncertainty need emergency care.

Also watch for
  • Sudden severe headache with no known cause
  • Sudden numbness or weakness, especially on one side of the body
  • Sudden confusion or trouble understanding speech
  • Sudden trouble walking or dizziness

Data & Privacy

Health logs and pulse summaries are stored in this browser. The FAST camera is a live mirror only; it does not analyze or save face images or video. Nutrition-label photos are processed locally and not retained. Barcode and food-search requests go to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. When you message the AI coach, your message, the chat so far, and (if you allow it) a summary of your logs are sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate a reply; chat history is kept in this browser. The barcode scanner, photo-recognition and OCR libraries load from public CDNs (unpkg, jsDelivr) the first time you use them, and fonts load from Google Fonts; photos themselves stay in this browser.

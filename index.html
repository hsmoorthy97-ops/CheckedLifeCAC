Recognize a stroke FAST

Call emergency services immediately if you notice ANY of these symptoms. Treatment works best when started quickly, so every minute counts.

Guided FAST check

If symptoms started suddenly, call 911 now. Do not wait to finish this guide. Check for face drooping, arm weakness, or unusual speech, and tap Concern for anything that seems off. If you are unsure, treat it as a concern.

Optional camera mirror (not a stroke test)Smile at the camera. Dots and guide lines across your face help you compare the left and right sides, like whether your mouth line stays level. The app does not decide whether your face looks normal, and a normal-looking face cannot rule out a stroke. Do not delay emergency care to use the camera.

  • Sudden numbness or weakness, especially on one side of the body

Data & Privacy

Health logs and pulse summaries are stored in this browser. The FAST camera mirror tracks face points on this device to draw guide dots; it never saves or uploads face images or video. Nutrition-label photos are processed locally and not retained. Barcode and food-search requests go to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. The barcode scanner, photo-recognition and OCR libraries load from public CDNs (unpkg, jsDelivr) the first time you use them, and fonts load from Google Fonts; photos themselves stay in this browser. Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

What CheckedLife stores

Your profile, food and workout logs, habits, medication reminders, pulse estimates and learned foods are saved only in this browser or app on your device. CheckedLife has no accounts server and does not collect, sell or share your data.

Your PIN

If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) version is stored on your device. It cannot be recovered if you forget it.

Camera and photos

Barcode scanning, food photos, nutrition-label reading, the pulse check and the FAST camera mirror are processed on your device. Photos and video are not saved or uploaded. For food learning, the app keeps a list of numbers describing a photo, not the photo itself.

Services the app contacts

When you look up a food, the barcode number or food name is sent to Open Food Facts or USDA FoodData Central to get nutrition information. The app also downloads code libraries (barcode, text-reading and AI models) from public content networks and fonts from Google Fonts. These services may see your device's IP address, as with any website.

Notifications

Medication reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

Use Clear All on the Report tab to delete everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data also removes it.

Children

CheckedLife does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because all data stays on the device.

Contact

Questions? Contact the CheckedLife team through our GitHub page.

Emergencies

If you or someone else may be having a stroke or any medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number right away. Do not rely on this app to decide whether to seek care.

Nutrition data comes from public databases and may contain errors. Food recognition, label reading and pulse estimates are approximate. Always check package labels and follow your clinician's advice.
